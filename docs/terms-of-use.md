## 3. Description of the Service
Catspace is a **mood diary for your cat**. You can log moods, notes, and photos, and review them on a calendar and in trends. Catspace also uses AI models to **detect cats in photos and suggest their mood**. Basic mode runs on your device. Pro mode sends the photo to our servers and a third-party AI provider for a more detailed analysis, as described in the Privacy Policy. Mood suggestions are **probabilistic suggestions based on a single photo** and may be inaccurate. Mood suggestions, diary entries, and insights do **not constitute veterinary, behavioral, or professional advice**. If you are concerned about your cat's health or behavior, please consult a veterinarian.

Your diary is stored on your device. We do not keep a copy, so we cannot restore it if it is lost, for example if you delete the App or lose your device.
